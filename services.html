Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How do you determine my flat rate quote?

We ask a few quick questions — home size, number of rooms, distance, and any specialty items (pianos, safes, etc.). Based on that we provide a firm, all-inclusive flat rate. No hourly surprises, no fuel surcharges.

Do you provide packing supplies?

Yes. Boxes, tape, bubble wrap, and moving blankets are all provided as part of our service. We wrap and protect everything before it leaves your door.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Metro Detroit including Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Detroit, Dearborn, Southfield, Troy, Sterling Heights, and surrounding cities. Not sure if we cover your area? Just call us.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Modern Moving Systems is fully licensed by the state of Michigan and carries comprehensive liability and cargo insurance. Your belongings are protected from pickup to delivery.

How does the monthly cleaning service work?

We schedule a recurring monthly visit at a time that works for you. Our crew performs a full deep clean and you're billed a flat $150 at the end of each month. No contracts, cancel any time.

How fast can you find me an apartment?

Most clients see their first options within 24–72 hours of submitting their criteria. We have an active network of landlords and property managers across Metro Detroit. Urgent moves are our specialty.

Do you do same-day or last-minute moves?

Yes, when availability allows. Call us directly at (313) 555-0192 for same-day or urgent bookings. We do our best to accommodate last-minute needs.

What if something gets damaged during the move?

We take every precaution to prevent damage, but in the rare event something is damaged, our insurance covers it. We stand behind our work 100% and will make it right.
