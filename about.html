Meet Moe

I started Modern Moving Systems in 2012 with one truck, one crew, and one simple promise: show up on time, treat people's belongings with respect, and charge a fair, honest price.

Back then, I saw how other companies nickeled and dimed customers — charging by the hour, adding fuel fees, showing up late, or not at all. I knew Metro Detroit deserved better. So I built it.

Over 12 years, we've moved more than 5,000 families across Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Detroit, and the broader Metro area. We expanded into apartment finding because I kept seeing the same problem — people moving to great cities but struggling to find the right place. Now we do both.

I'm proud that most of our business comes from referrals. When you treat people right, they tell their family and friends. That's the Detroit way.

What Drives Us

Built on Detroit
Values

Every decision we make goes back to three core beliefs that Moe built this company around.

Honesty First

The price we quote is the price you pay. No add-ons, no surprises, no excuses. We believe transparency builds lasting trust — and repeat customers.

Respect & Care

Your home and your belongings are sacred to you. We treat them that way — wrapping, protecting, and placing everything exactly where you want it.

Excellence Always

We don't cut corners. Whether it's a studio move or a full-house relocation, the same level of professionalism and effort goes into every single job.

Community Roots

We're not a national chain — we're a Metro Detroit company. We reinvest in our community, hire locally, and take pride in the cities we serve.

Reliability

Move day is stressful enough. When you book with us, we show up — on time, fully prepared, and ready to work. No-shows are not in our vocabulary.

5-Star Standard

Every job we take is an opportunity to earn a 5-star review. We hold ourselves to the highest standard so our reputation — and yours — stays spotless.

Our Journey

12 Years of Growth

The Beginning

Modern Moving Systems is Founded

Moe launches the company with one truck and a vision for honest, flat-rate moving in Metro Detroit. First month — 8 moves completed, all 5-star reviews.

Rapid Growth

Expanded to Ann Arbor & Ypsilanti

Demand from college students and young professionals drives us to officially expand beyond Detroit into Washtenaw County. Second truck added to the fleet.
